## 1. Final metadata

### App title (29 / 30 chars)
```
Word Search: Large Print Easy
```

It also matches the autocomplete phrase **"word search large print"** exactly, and Google Play combines title words, so it covers **"easy word search"** too. ## 10. Rating & review strategy

- **In-app review API** (`expo-store-review`) after positive moments only. Conditions: at least 3 sessions, at least 5 puzzles done, a 3-star finish or a streak milestone, at least 30 days since the last prompt, at most 3 lifetime prompts, and no ad in the last 60 s (details in DEVELOPMENT_PLAN §12).
- **No review gating** (do not ask "Do you like the app?" and then route only happy users to the store). Google policy discourages it.
- **Settings → "Send feedback"** email gives unhappy users a private channel before they leave a review.
- **Reply to every review in the first 90 days** within 48 h, in plain, warm language. For text-size or ad complaints, explain the setting or the change shipped.
- **Mine reviews weekly** for keywords seniors use ("big print", "easy on the eyes", "no timer") and feed them into description tests.
- Target: 4.5+ average and 100 ratings by week 6.

## 11. ASO iteration plan

**Week 0 (publish day)**
- In Applyra, add the app (`add_application`). Then `track_keywords` for: word search, word search for seniors, easy word search, word search large print, easy word search for seniors, word search offline, word puzzle games, word puzzle games for adults, word puzzle game for seniors, daily word challenge, letter grid puzzle, brain games for adults offline, word search hard.
- Add competitors: Vita Word Search for Seniors, tellmewow senior word search, playvalve Word Search, playsimple Word Search Explorer.

**Week 2: indexing and conversion baseline**
- Check `get_keyword_rank_history`: are we indexed for every tracked term? A term with no ranking means its placement needs strengthening.
- Play Console Store Listing Experiment #1: **icon A vs B** (50/50, until 90% confidence).
- Run `simulate_metadata` on alternative short description: *"Large print word search for seniors. Big letters, play offline, no timers."* and compare to current.

**Week 4: keyword expansion**
- If "word search for seniors" ranks in the top 10 and "word search" is outside the top 50, test title variant **"Word Search for Seniors: Easy"** (run `check_metadata` and then `simulate_metadata` first). Keep whichever version brings more organic installs per day, using a 14-day window for each.
- Listing experiment #2: **screenshot 1 caption** ("Big Letters. Easy to Read." vs "Large Print Word Search").
- Launch localized listings for es, pt-BR, de, fr (title and short description only) and watch impressions.

**Week 8: scale**
- Rewrite the full description using top review phrases and any new autocomplete long tails (`run_autocomplete` on "word search", "large print", "senior games").
- Re-run `get_aso_health` and `run_niche_analysis` for "word search" to find adjacent niches (e.g. "word find", "word search christmas") for seasonal description and screenshot swaps.
- Seasonal listing (October to December): screenshot 5 shows Halloween, Thanksgiving and Christmas packs, and a description line mentions holiday word search.

Always run `check_metadata` (GPLAY) on any new draft before you publish it.
