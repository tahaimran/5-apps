## One real flag: "No smoking" and "No alcohol" templates
The template library (category *Quit*) ships templates named "No smoking", "No alcohol", "No sugary snacks",
and onboarding offers the goal "Break a bad habit". They mention tobacco and alcohol in a cessation context,
and the IARC questionnaire asks whether the app *references* these substances. The plan expected an
Everyone / PEGI 3 result, but answering that question "yes" may produce a higher rating (for example a
PEGI 7 or Teen-style outcome in some regions).

Options:
1. Answer the questionnaire truthfully and accept the resulting rating (the app targets 13+ anyway, so this
   is low-impact), or
2. Rename the two templates to be substance-neutral ("No smoking" → "Stay smoke-free" still references
   tobacco, so a real change would be removing them) and re-run the questionnaire.

Do not answer "no" to make the rating lower; a mismatch can get the listing rejected. Record the final
answers and resulting ratings here.
