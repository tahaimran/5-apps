# Play Console → App content → Health apps declaration

| Question | Answer |
|---|---|
| Does the app have health features? | Yes: hydration (water intake) tracking and reminders |
| Feature type | Activity and fitness / Nutrition and weight management → "hydration tracking" (pick the closest option listed) |
| Is it a medical device, or does it diagnose, treat or prevent a condition? | **No** |
| Does it use Health Connect or health sensors? | **No** |
| Does it claim clinical or regulated outcomes? | **No**. Goal copy says "general wellness estimate, not medical advice" |

## Wording rules for the listing and the app
- Say "general wellness estimate". Never say "healthy hydration level", "prevents dehydration", "cures", "treats".
- No weight-loss promises. The ASO text mentions routines people already follow; keep it that way.
- Pregnancy, breastfeeding and senior modes are **not in this release** (v1.1). When they ship, they must
  show the "talk to your healthcare provider" banner (plan §8.1) and the declaration is re-checked.
- Sources behind the default goal formula (for reviewers who ask): EFSA 2010 adequate intake 2.0 L (women) /
  2.5 L (men); US NASEM 2004 total water; the common 30–35 ml/kg practitioner heuristic. The app applies a
  conservative 33 ml/kg plus activity and climate adjustments and clamps to 1.2–4.5 L.
