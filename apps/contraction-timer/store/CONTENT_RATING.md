## Topics to state honestly
The app is about pregnancy, labour and birth. Text mentions bleeding, waters breaking, pain, maternity pads,
breast pumps, sore breasts, C-sections and "reduced baby movements" in a factual, care-oriented way. None of this
is sexual content or nudity and there are no images of bodies (the week pictures are hand-drawn fruit and
everyday objects). If a question asks about "medical or health information", answer yes: general pregnancy
information, no diagnosis or treatment advice.

Expected result: **Everyone / PEGI 3** (plan §17). Record the final questionnaire answers and the resulting ratings
here after submission. Do not answer differently to get a lower rating.

Target audience and content: **18+ only**, not designed for children (WHATS_LEFT §4). The content rating and the
target audience are separate forms; an Everyone rating with an 18+ audience is normal.
